# Architecture Essentials

- **Tech Stack**: Android, Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3.
- **Core Components**: `VerseRepository` (Data), `AppNavigation` (Routing), `HomeScreen` & `ReaderScreen` (UI).
- **Styling**: Google Fonts (`ui-text-google-fonts`), Dark/Light modes configured in `Theme.kt` and `Color.kt`.
