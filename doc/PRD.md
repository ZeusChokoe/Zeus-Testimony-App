# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Purpose
The Zeus Testimony is a 7-volume foundational text that merges astrophysics with refactored mythology. It presents a scientific and philosophical treatise on Jupiter (Zeus) as the primordial architect of the solar system.

## 2. Target Users
- Readers interested in archaeoastronomy, mythology, and astrophysics.
- Enthusiasts of narrative-driven, lore-rich reading applications.

## 3. Major Features
- **7 Sacred Volumes**: Deep integration of the refactored Theogony into a "Bible-style" format.
- **Cross-Referencing**: Ability to link legacy myths to their scientific truths.
- **Epic UI/UX**: An immersive, 2026-standard reading environment with custom typography (Cinzel, Lora), rich deep-red color palettes, and sacred design elements.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: High-performance scrolling and state management via Jetpack Compose.
- **Maintainability**: Clear separation of data layer (`VerseRepository`) and UI layer.
