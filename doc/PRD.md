# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Purpose
The Zeus Testimony is a 12-volume foundational text that merges astrophysics with refactored mythology. It presents a scientific and philosophical treatise on Jupiter (Zeus) as the primordial architect of the solar system.

## 2. Target Users
- Readers interested in archaeoastronomy, mythology, and astrophysics.
- Enthusiasts of narrative-driven, lore-rich reading applications.

## 3. Major Features
- **12 Volumes, 49 chapters**: Deep integration of the refactored Theogony into a "Bible-style" format.
- **Labelled commentary**: Science, Myth, History and Wisdom notes attached to verses, so readers can tell established fact from hypothesis from story.
- **Epic UI/UX**: An immersive, 2026-standard reading environment with custom typography (Cinzel, Lora), rich deep-red color palettes, and sacred design elements.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements
- **Performance**: High-performance scrolling and state management via Jetpack Compose.
- **Maintainability**: Clear separation of data layer (`TestimonyRepository` + `assets/testimony.txt`) and UI layer.
